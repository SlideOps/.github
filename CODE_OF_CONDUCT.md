# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SlideOps to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment free community for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Our standards

Things that help the community thrive:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them find their footing.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback with good faith.
- Focusing on what is best for Operators and for the project.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including repositories, issues, pull requests, and any public space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to conduct@slideops.com. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban anyone who violates this code.

We adapt widely used community guidelines to fit SlideOps, and we may refine this document as the community grows.
