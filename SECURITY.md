# Security Policy

We take the security of SlideOps and of the infrastructure our Operators manage seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security reports. Instead, email us at security@sali-ai.com with:

- A description of the issue and the impact you believe it has.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept if you have one.
- Any relevant versions, configuration, or environment details.

You can expect an acknowledgement within a few business days. We will keep you updated as we investigate and work on a fix, and we will credit you if you would like once the issue is resolved.

## Scope

SlideOps operates on infrastructure over SSH and handles credentials, so we care especially about reports touching authentication, credential storage, remote execution, tenant isolation, and the approval and verification flow.

Thank you for helping keep Operators safe.
