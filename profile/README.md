## The Infrastructure Operations Platform

SlideOps helps Operators discover, configure, deploy, secure, verify, and monitor their own infrastructure through transparent, outcome driven workflows. You describe what you want to achieve. SlideOps understands the intent, plans the work, asks for your approval, carries it out over SSH, and proves the result.

Infrastructure stays yours. Knowledge becomes software. Confidence becomes the outcome.

> Knowledge becomes Capabilities. Capabilities create Confidence.

## What we are building

- An operational workspace organized around Capabilities rather than commands.
- A single, transparent lifecycle for every operation: discover, assess, recommend, plan, approve, execute, verify, observe, record.
- Broad Linux support from day one, across the common distributions and package managers.
- A modern experience that feels native on mobile as a progressive web app and equally considered on every other screen.

## What we are not

SlideOps is not a cloud provider, not a Linux distribution, and not a replacement for Docker, systemd, or Git. It orchestrates the tools you already rely on, explains what it is doing, and verifies that it worked.

## Principles

- Operators stay in control. Nothing runs without approval.
- Every execution is followed by verification and recorded in history.
- Automation stays transparent. No hidden actions.
- Simplicity and long term maintainability over novelty.

Built for developers, platform engineers, self hosters, home labs, startups, and teams who want to operate infrastructure with confidence.
